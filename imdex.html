Your learning journey

Step 1 · Build a base. Learn the Basic words — the everyday vocabulary you need first.
Step 2 · Speak daily. Practise everyday phrases in the Daily Phrases tab using practice mode.
Step 3 · Level up. Move to Intermediate and Proficient words to express ideas precisely.
Step 4 · Sound natural. Master Phrasal Verbs — natives use them constantly in real conversation.
Step 5 · Sound smart. Learn Idioms and Proverbs to add colour and wisdom to your speech.
Step 6 · Sound modern. Pick up common Slang to understand casual, informal English.
Step 7 · Speak correctly. Review Common Mistakes and Confusing Words to eliminate the errors that give you away.

Show header text & progress bar

Store progress in this browser
Save your learned words to local storage so they remain after you reload. Off by default to keep the browser light.
Auto-reset on browser / tab close
Clear all progress when you fully close the tab or browser. A normal page refresh keeps your progress.
Performance & data loading
Load first page of every tab at startup
When on, the first page of all sections is fetched as soon as the app opens. Off (default) loads only the tab you are viewing, and other tabs load on first visit.
Clear a tab's data when you switch away
When on, leaving a tab frees its loaded pages from memory and reloads them next time. Off (default) keeps them so revisits are instant.
Keep pages in memory while paginating
When on, Prev/Next accumulate pages (search & sort span all of them) and Prev never reloads. Off (default) keeps only the current page and reloads on navigation to stay light.
